# PersonaDrift Dataset Documentation

## Data Acquisition

### Sensors/modalities recorded
- **Audio**: Participant speech (captured via Furhat microphone array)
- **Text**: ASR transcriptions of participant utterances; LLM-generated robot responses
- **Non-verbal behaviour**: Furhat head gestures and facial expressions
- **Dialogue metadata**: Turn timestamps, elapsed time, token usage, LLM-generated emotion labels
- **System logs**: API response times, gesture execution logs

### Recording equipment and specifications
- **Robot platform**: Furhat
- **SDK version**: Furhat SDK 2.8.0]
- **Speech synthesis**: TTS ENGINE
- **ASR engine**: ASR ENGINE
- **LLM**: OpenAI GPT-4o-mini (Feb 2026 - April 2026)
- **Recording environment**: Quiet lab room

### Sample
- **Turn-level granularity**: All annotations at dialogue turn level
- **Timestamp precision**: Millisecond accuracy for turn boundaries
- **Gesture execution**: Real-time based on each turn of the dialogue

## Post-processing

### Cleaning, filtering, synchronization, or annotation steps
1. **ASR transcript cleanup**: Removed spurious annotations introduced during data export to spreadsheet format
2. **Token usage validation**: Flagged sessions where the `tokens_used` field was recorded as zero due to logging errors, with yellow highlights
3. **Gesture validation**: Identified and flagged discrepancies between LLM-requested gestures and available Furhat gestures with yellow highlights
4. **Manual anonymization**: Removed identifying names, locations, and personal information from transcripts to comply with ethics protocol
5. **Turn alignment**: Synchronized human and robot turns with elapsed time from session start
6. **Emotion label extraction**: Parsed structured LLM output to extract emotion field for each robot turn

### Annotation/coding scheme and inter-rater reliability
- **Emotion labels**: Predefined set of 9 discrete states (Engaged, Guarded, Defensive, Hopeful, Concerned, Frustrated, Relieved, Bored, Neutral) generated by LLM as part of structured output
- **Gesture mapping**: Deterministic emotion-to-gesture mapping (see Table 1 in paper); no human annotation required
- **Inter-rater reliability**: Not applicable (emotion and gesture labels are system-generated, not human-coded)

## Usage Notes
### Recommended and discouraged uses

**Recommended:**
- Studying persona consistency and drift in embodied LLM interactions
- Analyzing verbal–non-verbal alignment in social robots
- Developing prompt engineering strategies for stable persona behaviour
- Emotion recognition research in embodied dialogue systems
- Mixed-methods HRI studies combining qualitative and quantitative analysis

**Discouraged:**
- Treating LLM-generated emotion labels as psychological ground truth without validation
- Using diet-advising conversations as medical or clinical guidance
- Attempting to identify, contact, or profile participants
- Building production systems for health/clinical advice without clinical oversight
- Generalizing findings beyond this specific embodied system and context

### Utility and relevance to HRI research
PersonaDrift addresses a critical gap in embodied HRI datasets: turn-level alignment of LLM-generated dialogue, emotion, and gesture under fixed persona prompts. As LLM-driven social robots become more common, understanding persona stability and drift is essential for designing trustworthy, coherent long-term interactions. This dataset enables:
- Empirical measurement of persona drift in physical (not just simulated) interactions
- Development of drift detection and mitigation strategies
- Benchmarking persona consistency across different LLM architectures
- Mixed-methods analysis of how human questioning styles affect robot persona stability
